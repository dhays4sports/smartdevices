## What deserves attention

Cellular trackers differ in power source, location reporting, movement alerts, service coverage, concealment/tamper behavior, account privacy, and subscription. A tracker may provide context after unexpected movement; it does not prevent theft or guarantee recovery.

## Why it is relevant

Street parking raises the usefulness of unexpected-movement awareness. The stated OBD port and broad model-year range make OBD options worth verifying, not automatically compatible. Shared-driver understanding is relevant to location privacy. The no-subscription preference conflicts with the fully reviewed cellular options, so the plan shows the tradeoff instead of pretending a fit.
