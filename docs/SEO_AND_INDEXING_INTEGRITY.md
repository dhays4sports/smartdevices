# SEO, metadata, indexing, and structured-data integrity

The public sitemap includes the canonical `/insurance` directory, `/farmers`, public protection routes, institutional pages, and active device records. It excludes plan URLs, Pro workspace, APIs, and the `/insurance/farmers` alias. Robots rules disallow those noncanonical/private areas.

`/insurance` and `/farmers` include WebPage structured data under the SmartDevices.com WebSite identity. No structured relationship, endorsement, partnership, carrier ownership, eligibility, offer price, rating, or review schema is asserted. Metadata and structured data use the canonical SmartDevices host and 2026-08-26 content date.


## v5.3 ecosystem routes

`/connect` is a public explanatory/entry route and may be indexed as product documentation. Private registered device instances are never assigned public SEO pages or sitemap entries. `/api/devices/*` and `/api/capabilities` are machine-readable data surfaces, not synthetic SEO pages. Existing catalog device pages remain the canonical public device intelligence pages; SmartDevices is not represented as the manufacturer of third-party devices.
