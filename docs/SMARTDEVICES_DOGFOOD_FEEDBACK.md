# Business Builder dogfood evidence

Doctrine simplified this work: reuse existing decision flow and catalog instead of building universal device control, new registry or marketplace. Direct manufacturer intent tests a familiar revenue mechanism without agreements, payments or fulfillment. Implementation effort is not market validation. Generated UI is replaceable; evidence and durable project state are not.

Proven engineering lessons suitable for Mesh: explicitly distinguish browser-local saves from account persistence; a deployed site/service credential does not prove an authenticated owner journey; private synthetic counters cannot establish BB1 demand; capability matching must not imply installation fit or execution authority. These are demonstrated contract/inspection findings, not proven business economics.

Unproven: autonomous acquisition, positive contribution, low owner minutes, recurring data maintenance, willingness to pay. Do not elevate these hypotheses into registry defaults. Suggested general rule: physical-device information products can pass conditional ABE only when value delivery does not require the business to install, certify or operate hardware. Carry data-review burden explicitly.

Generalized lessons were proposed separately in dhays4sports/the-mesh on business-builder-physical-information-dogfood, stacked on autonomy PR #25. No product implementation or speculative economic claims moved into Mesh Core.
