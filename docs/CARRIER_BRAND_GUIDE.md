# Carrier brand guide

SmartDevices.com is always the primary identity. Carrier names are factual text references tied to governed evidence. Professional/agency identity is secondary. No carrier logo, trade dress, endorsement, “official,” approval, certification, or partnership language is permitted without a separate written authorization record.

## SmartDevices identity

The primary lockup pairs the lime smart-tile mark with the exact wordmark `SmartDevices.com`. The continuous S inside the tile represents a signal moving through a protected device. Use `smartdevices-logo-on-dark.svg` on dark surfaces, `smartdevices-logo-on-light.svg` on light surfaces, and `smartdevices-mark.svg` only when the full wordmark is already visible or the format is icon-sized. The favicon uses the same mark.

Keep the mark square, preserve its colors and clear space, and do not rotate, outline, recolor, add effects, separate the `.com`, place another identity above it, or combine it with a carrier or agency logo as one lockup. SmartDevices remains primary in the header, footer, plans, carrier guidance and Pro client views.

An authorization record must name the asset owner, exact files, route/component/size, territory, term/expiry, accessible alt text, permitted alterations, legal reviewer, revocation contact, cache purge process, and fallback. Expired or withdrawn authorization removes the asset immediately while preserving truthful text guidance. Logo authorization does not change evidence, eligibility, or product-fit states.
