# Carrier brand guide

SmartDevices.com is always the primary identity. Carrier names are factual text references tied to governed evidence. Professional/agency identity is secondary. No carrier logo, trade dress, endorsement, “official,” approval, certification, or partnership language is permitted without a separate written authorization record.

An authorization record must name the asset owner, exact files, route/component/size, territory, term/expiry, accessible alt text, permitted alterations, legal reviewer, revocation contact, cache purge process, and fallback. Expired or withdrawn authorization removes the asset immediately while preserving truthful text guidance. Logo authorization does not change evidence, eligibility, or product-fit states.
