# SMARTDEVICES-OUTCOME-FIRST-INTELLIGENCE-1.0 — Local adoption candidate

**Status:** Project-specific strategic operating lens; documentation only. No hosted or physical certification.  
**Portfolio doctrine:** [The Mesh canonical outcome-first standard](https://github.com/dhays4sports/the-mesh/blob/main/docs/PORTFOLIO_OUTCOME_FIRST_INTELLIGENCE_1.0.md).

## Customer outcome

Help a person understand a practical physical-world concern, **shop and compare** credible compatible options, make a reasoned plan, and—only when appropriately integrated and authorized—connect or operate devices and verify the result.

Preserve DISCOVER -> CONNECT -> CREATE -> OPERATE as the experience North Star; make the value and compatibility of each selected component evident. An attractive index, recommendation, "scan" or generated plan is not evidence that protection improved or a device was installed.

## Candidate measures

- Consumer comprehension / plan completion / deliberate selection (measured, not presumed).
- Verified compatibility evidence, fresh citations, disclosed assumptions, known product/insurance limits.
- Confirmed opt-in handoff and actual completion of setup when supported.
- Integration reliability, owner effort, provider cost; later physical-device outcome only with separately approved verification.

## Roadmap filter

| Scope | Disposition | Evidence / stop condition |
|---|---|---|
| Existing catalog, explanations, comparison, Smart Safety Plan and provider-neutral disclosures | KEEP | Accuracy, accessibility, causal clarity and honest limits |
| Authenticated save/reopen and isolated hosting validation | PROVE NEXT | Distinct test data, durable project reload, auth A/B isolation, rollback evidence, /farmers regression |
| Evidenced product-content update/publication | PROVE NEXT under existing draft gates | Immutable reviewed batch, source read-set, signer authorization, verification and rollback |
| Device operation, installer authority, insurance discounts and acceptance | DEFER / STOP until verified | No controls or coverage guarantees without device, owner, insurer and safety certification |
| Generic IoT platform or separate CRM | DEFER | No demonstrated customer outcome requiring it |

Maintain carrier-neutral `/insurance`, California-specific `/farmers` pilot and existing external activation boundaries. Producer/insurer acceptance is distinct from recommendations, purchase, installation and verification. Don't activate real data, credentials, devices, payments, partner communications, autopublishing, or public deployments under this mandate.

## Required design/PR record

Define buyer and job; expected consumer outcome; reliable source evidence; compatibility and failure cases; baseline and task-success observation; unit economics/human support burden; control and data permissions; why reuse is insufficient; browse/compare/configure UX; falsification gate; exact validation maturity (mock/local/isolated/live). Missing information is **UNKNOWN**.
