# Device Identity + Registry Boundary

## deviceregistry.org

Strategic role: normalized registry/infrastructure boundary for device records, capabilities, compatibility, provenance, discovery, registration and trust state.

Current status: **contract defined / local foundation only**. This repository does not claim that `deviceregistry.org` is hosted, production-active or authoritative.

## device.eth

Strategic role: optional portable namespace / durable device identity edge.

Current status: **North Star / planned integration**. No live binding is activated by this reconciliation.

A namespace association is not proof of:

- physical ownership;
- physical control;
- manufacturer authenticity;
- safety/certification;
- permission;
- agent authority.

## Relationship model

Device identity must remain distinct from human, organization, agent and bot identity. Relationships are explicit:

- principal may assert a control/ownership claim;
- evidence may verify a claim;
- a device may receive an identity reference;
- a permission may authorize a principal or agent for a bounded capability;
- revocation may terminate that authorization independently of identity.

The registry stores/query relationships; Mesh may provide stronger identity/authority/execution rails where appropriate.
