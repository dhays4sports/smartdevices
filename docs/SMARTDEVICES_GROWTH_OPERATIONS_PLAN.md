# Growth operations plan

Current baseline is unknown; this is a bounded operating plan, not a claim that growth automation runs. No schedule, outreach, paid campaign or public launch activated.

| Loop | Objective / source | Opportunity and cadence | Action / boundary | Success / stop | Owner target min/week |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Search intent | Qualified water-planning visits; Search Console | Weekly, once property access exists; query with impressions and poor useful landing | Improve one existing evidence-backed page; publication approval until policy exists | Planning actions rise; stop page expansion if no intent signal | 15 |
| Plan usefulness | Completed local plan + manufacturer intent; event aggregates | Weekly after non-test cohort authorized | Remove one confusing step, preserve scope | Repeated plan/action behavior; redesign if visits do not progress | 15 |
| Data freshness | Accurate shortlist; dated source records | Weekly due/changed claims | Retrieve official source, diff, propose supervised patch; never auto-verify contradictions | No unsupported capability claims; park record on conflict | 30 |
| Link health | Usable manufacturer destination | Weekly; redirect/error trigger | Repair only against verified source; no arbitrary redirect monetization | Working links; stop broken destination | 10 |
| Support/exceptions | Low recurring attention; issue log | Weekly summary, incident immediate | Public guidance fixes; no household consulting | <=120 total owner minutes/week MVB | 15 |
| Economics/governance | Net value after owner cost | Monthly after real signals | Decide referral enrollment or redesign; no agreement signed automatically | Positive contribution with measured labor; stop if structurally negative | 10 |

Initial scope limit: one water-leak entry and existing records, not mass SEO pages. Establish 30 days of authorized non-test observation or 100 qualified visits (whichever takes longer; reassess after 60 days without acquisition). These are proposed evaluation windows, not empirical benchmarks. Require repeated independent planning and outbound behavior before affiliate onboarding; no fixed optimistic conversion hurdle. Never count private test traffic.

If discovery is used but Builder is not, focus directory/comparison. If plans drive manufacturer intent, prefer referral over subscription. If compatibility is the job, invest there before identity. If scope needs manufacturer deals/live controls to matter, VENTURE_PARK. Broadening pages without fresh evidence is disallowed.

SEO: retain real device/category/capability routes, metadata and source visibility; private pilot noindex, robots disallow and empty sitemap remain. No new mass pages, ranking promises or artificial reviews. Field performance, indexing and production canonical behavior still need measured audits.
