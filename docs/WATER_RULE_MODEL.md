# California Farmers water rule model

The public Farmers pages support two independent facts: a broad potential plumbing leak detection/shutoff category, and a current California public presentation involving Moen Flo. Neither creates a universal requirement or individual discount/eligibility decision.

If a consumer or professional says a requirement exists, that assertion renders first and remains labeled by actor. The engine then shows current public category evidence, exact technical capability fit, independent SmartDevices guidance, and confirmation items. Point detection, whole-home monitoring, and automatic main-line shutoff are non-substitutable capability IDs.

Every water result asks the consumer or professional to confirm policy applicability, model, pipe size/material/location, power and Wi-Fi, installation, activation, function check, maintenance, and documentation. Unknown or mismatched capability detail produces “Confirmation needed,” not a weaker substitute.
