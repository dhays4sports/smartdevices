# Pro literal engagement states

Generated, shared-by-adapter, viewed, client-responded, and archived are distinct plan states. Delivery becomes shared-by-adapter only after an activated provider accepts the request. Rejection, timeout, suppression, consent withdrawal, expiration, revocation, and unavailable providers do not advance the plan.

A view records only that the route was opened. It does not assert interest, selection, purchase, installation, evidence, professional review, carrier determination, or verification. The in-memory adapter exists only for deterministic tests; the production adapter remains disabled.
