# Sample Smart Safety Plan — Home Water

Schema: plan v2 · sample provenance only · no client identity. Scenario answers: property often empty; automatic response preferred where supported; professional installation acceptable; compare subscription models; Wi-Fi certainty unknown.

## What deserves attention

Unusual water activity can develop while nobody is near the source. Point sensors can add local awareness; main-line systems can add whole-home patterns and supported automatic shutoff.

## Why it is relevant

Frequent time away makes remote awareness more relevant. The stated automatic-response preference raises main-line shutoff options. Professional installation is acceptable. Wi-Fi remains unknown, so connectivity fit is a visible verification item rather than an inferred fact.

## Prioritized options

1. Strong fit: Moen Flo Smart Water Monitor and Shutoff — whole-home monitoring and supported shutoff; professional plumbing installation; hardware, installation, current service terms, plumbing fit, power, and Wi-Fi must be checked.
2. Worth comparing: Phyn Plus (2nd Gen) — pressure-wave monitoring and supported shutoff; professional plumbing installation; hardware was in a mid/high price band when reviewed; adapter/plumbing/network fit remains unverified.
3. Optional point layer: Kidde Water Leak + Freeze Detector — point detection near a water heater or appliance; placement and alert connectivity matter; it does not close the main supply.

## Cost, setup, service, maintenance, compatibility, privacy, and insurance

Main-line shutoff options generally add professional plumbing cost and may offer optional or changing service terms. Point sensors are simpler but cover only their placement. Confirm line size/location, power, Wi-Fi, app/account data practices, alert recipients, testing, firmware, batteries where applicable, and current manufacturer terms. Ask the user's own carrier or licensed professional whether documentation is required; no eligibility, discount, approval, prevention, or claim outcome is promised.
