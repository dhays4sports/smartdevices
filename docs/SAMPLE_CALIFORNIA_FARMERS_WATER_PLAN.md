## Options and decision considerations

| Area | Plan guidance |
|---|---|
| Solution class | Whole-home monitoring with automatic main-line shutoff is distinct from a point leak sensor. A point sensor may still be useful independently but does not meet the stated shutoff capability. |
| Device observation | Moen Flo is the only product tied to the current governed California public-offer evidence. This is not a Farmers approval or eligibility determination. |
| Cost | Current purchase price, taxes, plumbing changes, permits, professional labor, and any service cost must be confirmed directly; no price is represented as current in this sample. |
| Setup and compatibility | Confirm pipe size/material/orientation, valve and power location, Wi-Fi, account/phone support, fire-suppression or irrigation branches, local code/permit conditions, and manufacturer restrictions before selection. |
| Installation | Use a qualified plumbing professional where required. Do not interrupt protected fire-suppression service or assume every main-line configuration can accept the device. |
| Subscription and outages | Confirm which alerts/history/service features require an account or paid service and how shutoff/monitoring behaves during power, Wi-Fi, internet, or cloud outage. |
| Maintenance | Keep manufacturer-directed testing, battery/power/network checks, valve exercise, firmware/account review, and installer records. A connected device does not replace property inspection or emergency action. |
| Privacy/security | Review household access, account recovery, multi-factor authentication where offered, telemetry/cloud retention, sharing, and removal of access when occupants or installers change. |
| Insurance/documentation | Ask Farmers or the licensed agent whether the specific policy has a requirement, offer, potential category, installation standard, accepted proof, or follow-up interval. Do not treat installation or this plan as carrier determination. |
