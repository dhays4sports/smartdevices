# SmartDevices MVB / MAB / Autonomy Contract

Status: accepted under SD-BB-0 · 2026-10-02

## Purpose

Prevent SmartDevices from mistaking a technically impressive platform for a functioning business.

## Minimum Viable Business (MVB)

MVB requires an external customer/design partner and a real commercial commitment.

Required evidence:

- supported customer problem;
- recorded Buy / Adapt / Build decision;
- accepted priced offer for a Validated Build Pack;
- payment or contractually binding commitment through an approved process;
- delivered Build Pack with literal validation evidence;
- physical implementation of the promised bounded function;
- discrepancy/correction lineage;
- delivery/receipt record;
- measured owner intervention;
- measured direct cost and gross economics.

A self-funded internal prototype is **BB7 evidence**, not MVB revenue.

## Minimum Autonomous Business (MAB)

MAB requires repeatable operations, not merely a second customer.

Routine supported jobs should be able to move through:

`ACQUIRE → QUALIFY → RESEARCH → PRICE → COLLECT → GENERATE → VALIDATE → DELIVER → SUPPORT → MONITOR → RECOVER`

under explicit policies and bounded provider/tool authority.

## Human-required boundaries

Human review remains required for:

- blocked/review-required physical safety classes;
- uncertain engineering outside supported templates;
- material provider/vendor contract changes;
- capital allocation;
- unapproved pricing exceptions;
- high-value/irreversible manufacturing commitments;
- legal/compliance decisions;
- constitutional/business-model changes.

## Required business metrics

Track from SD-001 onward:

- Owner Intervention Hours;
- Intervention Rate;
- Autonomous Gross Profit Efficiency;
- request → decision time;
- paid request → validated delivery time;
- Build Pack rebuild success;
- hidden/manual engineering steps;
- revisions per completed build;
- support minutes per project;
- direct cost and gross margin per build;
- Buy/Adapt/Build distribution;
- percent of Build requests correctly redirected to existing products;
- provider failure/recovery rate.

## Initial MAB targets

Internal targets only; revise from observed MVB data:

- ≥80% of routine in-scope workflow events complete without owner intervention;
- median owner intervention ≤20 minutes for a repeat supported Build Pack, excluding separately sold engineering review;
- 100% of paid deliveries have project/revision/validation/receipt records;
- provider failures have bounded retry/fallback/escalation behavior;
- no expansion of safety/payment authority to improve the metric.

## Business-health rule

Revenue growth does not excuse rising founder dependence.

A recurring-profit business with low intervention may be more valuable than a higher-revenue business that requires continuous bespoke engineering.
