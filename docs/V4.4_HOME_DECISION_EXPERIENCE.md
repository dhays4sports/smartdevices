## Consumer flow

- Homepage Home action opens `/protect/home` directly. Other environments retain their existing experience.
- Three explicit entry choices: help choosing, insurance guidance, and known-device research.
- Six Home concern buttons with equivalent optional house hotspots. Reuses the existing licensed/project house illustration; no new decorative assets or copied carrier branding.
- Water distinguishes point detection, whole-home monitoring and automatic main-line shutoff. A point sensor is excluded from the shutoff match set.
- Immediate bounded options with dated equipment costs, separate unknown installation costs, subscriptions, availability, limitations and source links.
- Unknown permission and connectivity remain visible. Optional answers stay only in the local plan; shared URLs and public exports exclude them.
- Save/open a comparison plan, select a next step, keep local self-reported progress, download a readable text summary, and print/save PDF.
- My Plans reopens the same plan. Shared links can be explicitly saved into the recipient browser's index. When storage fails, no persistence is claimed; a current-visit summary remains available.

## Agent flow and boundaries

Home plans prepared in existing Pro open the focused Home decision surface, not the general homepage. Full carrier provenance and original plan detail remain in a secondary disclosure. A link's professional-stated label does not authenticate its sender or establish a requirement. No additional policy facts or private professional notes are put into a public URL.

The current release does not activate cross-device professional identity, delivery, appointments, purchase feeds, evidence uploads, carrier determination or a Coverage House return integration. Customers can manually share their downloaded summary. Production Pro remains protected by its existing server-side authorization. Device selection, purchased-self-reported, installation-scheduled and installed-self-reported are not professional review or carrier approval.

## Evidence

All device facts still come from the current published Evidence Autopilot snapshot with the existing file fallback. The new surface does not renew dates or infer fresh prices. It suppresses active catalog matches when a linked source is restricted, expired, conflicting or otherwise noncurrent, or when the dated record is more than 90 days old. Previously saved stale records remain readable with an explicit review warning.
