# Institutional independence and brand continuity

SmartDevices.com is the primary platform identity across the homepage, insurance directory, California Farmers page, Smart Safety Plans, and SmartDevices Pro. The inherited “The Future Has An Address.” language, manifesto, SmartDevices Index, strategic-partnership access, and protected original SVGs remain unchanged. Carrier and professional names are contextual/secondary.

The California pilot uses text references only. No Farmers logo, trade dress, endorsement, ownership, certification, or official-partnership treatment is active. SmartDevices' product facts and recommendations remain independent of carrier rules. Future carriers enter through governed registry/evidence/configuration and a carrier content adapter; they do not fork the product shell or generic plan contracts.

Carrier-specific routes must show visible scope/date/independence disclosure and useful generic recovery outside verified jurisdictions. Logo or co-marketing assets require an append-only authorization record defining owner, exact asset, placement, term, territory, permitted modifications, accessibility treatment, withdrawal process, and reviewer approval before publication.
